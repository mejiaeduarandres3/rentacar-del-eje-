Car Rental in Villamaría, Colombia — Your Gateway to Nevado del Ruiz

Villamaría is a municipality in the department of Caldas located just 15 minutes from Manizales, the departmental capital. Though it's a small and quiet town, its geographic position makes it the most direct gateway to Parque Nacional Natural Los Nevados and Nevado del Ruiz, one of Colombia's most iconic active volcanoes. From the town center of Villamaría, at roughly 1,920 meters elevation, the road climbs dramatically to over 5,000 meters at the park's summit, passing through páramo grasslands, cloud forests, and volcanic landscapes of extraordinary beauty.

To drive this route you need a 4x4 vehicle. The road up to Nevado del Ruiz has unpaved sections, steep grades, and rapidly changing weather conditions that can go from heavy rain to thick fog. A high-clearance vehicle with four-wheel drive, such as the Toyota Fortuner, is the safe and comfortable choice for ascending without trouble. Our fleet has this vehicle available for rental, ideal for this high-mountain adventure.

Beyond Nevado del Ruiz, from Villamaría you can access the Termales del Ruiz, natural hot springs located in the mountains at over 3,500 meters elevation. The road to the hot springs also requires a high-clearance vehicle, but the reward is worth it: pools of volcanic hot water surrounded by páramo and spectacular views. It's a unique experience that very few destinations in Colombia can offer.

For those not planning to head up into the mountains, Villamaría is also a strategic base. Its proximity to Manizales (just 15 minutes by car) lets you enjoy the gastronomy, cultural life, and viewpoints of the Caldas capital without the complications of staying in a big city. Villamaría offers more affordable and peaceful lodging options, with easy access to the entire department of Caldas and the rest of the Coffee Region.

Our car rental service in Villamaría includes home delivery to your hotel or accommodation. All vehicles come with valid SOAT (mandatory insurance), comprehensive coverage, and unlimited mileage. We accept international driver's licenses, payments in USD and Colombian pesos, and we don't require a credit card. Book via WhatsApp and we'll coordinate delivery in Villamaría or Manizales.
