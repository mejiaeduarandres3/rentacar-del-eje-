Car Rental in Circasia, Quindío — Explore the Coffee Region from Its Center

Circasia is a quiet and charming municipality located in the heart of Quindío, just 15 minutes from Armenia and another 15 from Salento. Its name comes from the Caucasus region, and today it is known for its lively main plaza, its famous Free Cemetery — one of the few in Colombia where the graves carry no religious symbols — and for being an excellent connection point between the department's most popular tourist towns.

Staying in Circasia is a smart choice for travelers seeking tranquility without losing access to the main attractions. From here, Salento and the Cocora Valley are 15 minutes away, Filandia is 20 minutes, Montenegro and the Parque Nacional del Cafe are 15 minutes, and Armenia with its airport is 15 minutes. With a rental car, you can visit a different town each day without needing to wake up early to catch a bus.

The town has a main plaza with cafes and restaurants where you can sit and watch the afternoon go by, artisanal ice cream shops, and souvenir stores. On the outskirts, there are coffee farms offering specialty coffee tours, and the rural area is perfect for walks through coffee plantations and bamboo groves. Having your own car in Circasia lets you combine the best of small-town life with the freedom to explore the entire region.
