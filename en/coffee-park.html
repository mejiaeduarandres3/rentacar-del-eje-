How to Get to the Coffee Park by Car

From Armenia (El Edén Airport)

18 km via the Armenia–Montenegro road. Paved and well-signposted. Estimated time: 20 minutes. Pick up your car at El Edén Airport.

From Pereira (Matecaña Airport)

55 km via the Pereira–Armenia highway. Paved road with good signage. Estimated time: 45 minutes. Pick up at Matecaña Airport.

From Salento

35 km via the Salento–Armenia road and then south to Montenegro. Paved road. Estimated time: 40 minutes. A scenic drive through coffee country.

What to See & Do at the Coffee Park

Rides & Roller Coasters

The Coffee Park offers more than 20 attractions including roller coasters, water rides, a free-fall tower, and a cable car that glides over the entire park. The roller coaster is one of the longest in Latin America and delivers genuine thrills surrounded by green mountains.

  • Admission fee applies — check current prices before visiting
  • Any vehicle can access the park

Coffee Culture Museum

Walk through interactive exhibits that trace the history of Colombian coffee from seed to cup. You will see traditional processing equipment, learn about the different coffee-growing regions of Colombia, and understand why Quindío coffee is considered among the world's finest. The museum also showcases the cultural traditions of the coffee-growing families who shaped this region.

Live Shows & Performances

Throughout the day, the park hosts live music, dance performances, and theatrical shows celebrating Colombian folklore. The main amphitheater features colorful presentations that bring the story of coffee and rural Colombian life to the stage. Show schedules rotate seasonally, so check at the entrance when you arrive.

Gardens & Botanical Trail

The park is set within lush tropical gardens featuring native orchids, bamboo groves, and coffee plantations you can walk through. A botanical trail winds through the grounds, making it a pleasant experience even if you skip the rides. Ideal for visitors who want a calmer pace alongside the amusement attractions.

Why Drive? Arrive Early, Stay Late

With a rental car from Armenia you control your schedule entirely. Arrive when the gates open to enjoy the popular rides with shorter queues, take a lunch break at your own pace, and stay until closing without watching the clock for a tour bus departure. You can also combine your visit with PANACA, just 20 minutes away by car.

Practical Information

Parking

The Coffee Park has a large parking lot right at the entrance. Your car will be safe while you enjoy the park all day.

Weather

Montenegro sits at 1,300 m elevation. Average temperature 22–28°C (72–82°F). Warm and pleasant. Bring sunscreen and a hat. Rain is possible, so a light rain jacket is wise.

Food & Drinks

Several restaurants and snack stands inside the park serve traditional Colombian food. You can also find restaurants along the Armenia–Montenegro road.

Best Time to Visit

Open year-round. Weekdays are quieter than weekends and holidays. Driest months: December–February and June–August. Check current hours before visiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a 4x4 to get to the Coffee Park?

No. All roads to the Coffee Park are paved and in excellent condition. Any vehicle in our fleet will get you there without issues.

How long should I plan for a visit?

Plan a full day (6–8 hours) to enjoy all the rides, shows, and coffee exhibits. Arriving early helps you avoid crowds and make the most of your visit.

Can I combine the Coffee Park with PANACA?

Yes! PANACA is only about 20 minutes away by car. However, each park deserves a full day, so we recommend visiting them on separate days if possible.

Do you accept international driving licenses?

Yes. We accept international driving licenses from any country, including all EU nations. We also accept payments in USD and offer bilingual service.

What is the advantage of driving to the Coffee Park?

With your own car you arrive early, avoid tour bus crowds, leave whenever you want, and can easily combine with PANACA, Filandia, or Salento on the same trip.
