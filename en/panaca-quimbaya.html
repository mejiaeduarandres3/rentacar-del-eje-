How to Get to PANACA by Car

From Armenia (El Edén Airport)

22 km via Armenia–Montenegro–Quimbaya road. Paved and well-signposted. Estimated time: 25 minutes. Pick up your car at El Edén Airport.

From Pereira (Matecaña Airport)

60 km via Pereira–Alcalá–Quimbaya road. Paved highway. Estimated time: 50 minutes. Pick up at Matecaña Airport.

From Coffee Park / Montenegro

12 km north on the Montenegro–Quimbaya road. Paved and easy to drive. Estimated time: 20 minutes. A natural addition to a Coffee Park day trip.

What to See & Do at PANACA

Interactive Animal Stations

PANACA is home to more than 4,500 animals across themed stations. Visitors can interact with horses, goats, pigs, rabbits, birds, and many other species in a hands-on environment. Each station is designed so you can touch, feed, and learn about the animals rather than just observe them from behind a fence.

  • Admission: $77,000–$98,000 COP/adult (~$19–$24 USD) depending on passport type (full day or 2-day with night horse show) — children under 90 cm free, kids 90 cm–12 years pay children's rate
  • Ticket office: 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. — open daily in high season; closed Wednesdays in low season
  • Night horse show: select dates from 6:15 p.m. (separate experience)
  • Any vehicle can access the park

Horse & Livestock Shows

Throughout the day, PANACA hosts live shows featuring trained horses, herding dogs, and livestock displays. The equestrian shows are a highlight, showcasing Colombia's Paso Fino horses with skilled riders demonstrating traditional riding techniques. Show schedules vary, so check at the entrance when you arrive.

Rural Colombian Culture

PANACA offers a window into traditional Colombian farm life. Walk through working coffee and sugarcane demonstrations, watch artisans making panela (raw cane sugar), and learn how rural communities in the Eje Cafetero have lived for generations. It is an educational experience wrapped in entertainment, giving visitors genuine insight into agricultural Colombia. Nearby coffee farms like Finca El Jazmín in Quimbaya (traditional architecture, authentic atmosphere, just minutes from PANACA) or Finca El Ocaso and Las Acacias in Salento make a perfect complement to this visit.

Adventure Activities

Beyond the animals, PANACA offers adventure activities including zip-lines, canopy tours, and horseback riding through the park's green grounds. The park is set on rolling hills with beautiful views of the Quindío countryside, making every walk between stations a scenic experience in itself.

Perfect for Families

PANACA is widely considered the most family-friendly attraction in the Coffee Region. Children love the direct contact with animals, the pony rides, and the interactive stations. With a rental car from Armenia, families arrive on their own schedule, carry strollers and supplies easily, and combine the visit with the nearby Coffee Park on another day.

Practical Information

Admission & Hours

$77,000–$98,000 COP/adult (~$19–$24 USD) depending on passport type (full day, or 2-day with night horse show). Children under 90 cm enter free; kids 90 cm–12 years pay children's rate. Ticket office: 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Open daily in high season; closed Wednesdays in low season. Night horse show on select dates from 6:15 p.m.

Parking

PANACA has a large parking area at the entrance. Your car will be safe while you explore the park all day.

Weather

Quimbaya sits at 1,300 m elevation. Average temperature 22–28°C (72–82°F). Warm and pleasant. Bring sunscreen and comfortable walking shoes.

Food

Restaurants inside the park serve traditional Colombian meals. You will also find snack stands throughout. Quimbaya town has additional dining options on your way back.

Best Time to Visit

Open year-round. Weekdays are much quieter. Driest months: December–February and June–August. Check current hours before visiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a 4x4 to get to PANACA?

No. All roads to PANACA are paved and in good condition. Any vehicle in our fleet will get you there without issues.

How long should I plan for a visit?

Plan a full day (6–8 hours) to see all the animal stations, watch the shows, and enjoy the interactive experiences.

Is PANACA good for kids?

Absolutely. PANACA is one of the best family attractions in the Coffee Region. Children love the direct contact with animals, pony rides, and interactive stations. It is ideal for all ages.

Can I combine PANACA with the Coffee Park?

Yes! The Coffee Park in Montenegro is only about 20 minutes away by car. We recommend visiting each park on a separate day so you can enjoy both fully.

How much does admission to PANACA cost?

Admission is $77,000–$98,000 COP per adult (~$19–$24 USD) depending on the passport type (full day, or 2-day with night horse show). Children under 90 cm enter free; kids 90 cm to 12 years pay a children’s rate. Ticket office: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Open daily in high season; closed Wednesdays in low season.

Do you accept international driving licenses?

Yes. We accept international driving licenses from any country, including all EU nations. We also accept payments in USD and offer bilingual service.
