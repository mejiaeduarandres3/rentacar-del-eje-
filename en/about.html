About Us

Your trusted car rental partner in Colombia's Coffee Region

Who We Are

Renta Car del Eje is a car rental company based in Colombia's Coffee Region (Eje Cafetero). Since 2019, we've been helping Colombian and international travelers explore Pereira, Armenia, Manizales, and the surrounding coffee country with complete freedom.

With over 7 years of experience and a fleet of 7 late-model vehicles — from Toyota Fortuner 4x4s to compact Kia city cars — all our vehicles are in excellent mechanical condition with current SOAT insurance and full coverage.

For International Visitors

We welcome tourists from around the world. We accept international driving permits and licenses from your home country, offer flexible payment in USD, EUR, and Colombian pesos, and provide English-speaking support via WhatsApp 24/7. Free delivery to all three regional airports: Matecaña (PEI), El Edén (AXM), and La Nubia (MZL).

Rental Policies

Clear terms for your peace of mind

Required Documents

Colombians: National ID + valid driver's license.
Foreigners: Valid passport + international or home-country driver's license.

Insurance & Deposit

All vehicles include current SOAT insurance and full coverage. A refundable security deposit is required and returned when the vehicle is returned in the same condition.

Delivery & Return

Free delivery to airports, hotels, or any address in the Coffee Region. Vehicles are delivered with a full tank and should be returned in the same condition.

Payment Methods

Cash (COP, USD, EUR), bank transfer, Nequi, and Daviplata. Booking is confirmed with a 1-day rental deposit.
