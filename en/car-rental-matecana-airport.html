Car Rental at Matecaña Airport Pereira — Your Gateway to the Coffee Region

Matecaña International Airport (IATA code: PEI) is the main airport in Colombia's Coffee Region, located just 10 minutes from downtown Pereira in the department of Risaralda. It operates daily domestic flights from Bogota, Medellin, and Cali, and seasonally receives international flights from Panama and the United States, making it the most important entry point for tourists visiting the Eje Cafetero.

Our car rental service at Matecaña Airport works simply: when you book via WhatsApp, send us your flight number and arrival time. We monitor your flight in real time so we're ready when you land, regardless of delays. We meet you at the terminal exit with your vehicle parked in the airport lot. The handover takes less than 10 minutes: sign the contract, do a quick walkaround of the car, and you're on your way.

Having your own car from the airport makes an enormous difference when exploring the Coffee Region. Instead of relying on taxis or intercity buses with fixed schedules, you can drive straight to your accommodation in Pereira, head to the famous Santa Rosa de Cabal hot springs (40 minutes), continue to the charming town of Salento and the Cocora Valley (1 hour 15 minutes), or climb up to Manizales (1 hour) to explore Nevado del Ruiz. All at your own pace, with no waiting and no distance surcharges.

Our fleet includes vehicles for every budget and need. For families or groups planning to explore rural roads to coffee farms and the Cocora Valley, we recommend the Toyota Fortuner or the Toyota Hilux, both 4x4 vehicles ideal for unpaved roads. For couples or travelers staying on paved highways, the Kia Seltos or Chevrolet Tracker offer comfort, great fuel economy, and easy parking in the small towns.

All our vehicles include valid SOAT (mandatory insurance), comprehensive coverage, and unlimited mileage. We accept international driver's licenses from any country, payments in US dollars, euros, and Colombian pesos. No credit card required. Our service operates 24/7, including late-night flights and holidays. Book now via WhatsApp and secure your vehicle at Matecaña Airport in Pereira.
