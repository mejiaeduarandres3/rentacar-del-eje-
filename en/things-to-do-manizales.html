How to Get to Manizales by Car

From La Nubia Airport (Manizales)

8 km to the city center. Paved road. Estimated time: 15 minutes. Pick up your car at La Nubia Airport.

From Pereira

55 km via the Pereira–Manizales highway through Chinchina. Paved road with mountain curves. Estimated time: 1 hour 30 minutes. Pick up at Matecaña Airport.

From Armenia

From Chinchina

25 km on the Chinchina–Manizales road. Paved mountain road with stunning views of coffee plantations. Estimated time: 30 minutes.

What to See & Do in Manizales

Cathedral of Manizales

The Cathedral Basílica of Our Lady of the Rosary is one of the tallest churches in Latin America, towering 113 meters above the central square. Built in neo-Gothic style between 1928 and 1939, the cathedral features striking concrete construction and an observation tower that offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the city and surrounding mountains. The interior is equally impressive, with soaring arches and stained glass windows that flood the nave with colored light.

  • Tower access: admission fee applies
  • Located at the main square (Plaza de Bolívar)

Cable Aéreo (Gondola)

The Cable Aéreo is a gondola system connecting Manizales with the neighboring town of Villamaría. The ride offers stunning aerial views of the city, the green valleys below, and on clear days, the snow-capped peak of Nevado del Ruiz. It is both a local transportation system and a scenic experience worth doing purely for the views. The gondola runs frequently, and the full journey takes about 20 minutes each way.

Chipre Viewpoint

The Chipre neighborhood sits on a ridge at the northern edge of Manizales and offers one of the most dramatic viewpoints in the Coffee Region. On a clear morning you can see the full sweep of the Andes, including Nevado del Ruiz and Nevado del Tolima. The area has cafes and restaurants where you can sit with a coffee and take in the view. Drive up in your rental car for the most convenient access.

Eco-Park Los Yarumos

An ecological park within the city limits, Los Yarumos offers walking trails through native cloud forest, canopy bridges, and ziplines. It is a green retreat from the urban bustle, accessible in minutes by car. The park preserves an important patch of Andean forest and is home to native birds and butterflies you will not spot anywhere else in the city.

Why Drive in Manizales?

Manizales is built on steep mountain ridges, and many of its best attractions are spread across neighborhoods connected by winding roads. A rental car lets you move freely between Chipre, the Cathedral district, Los Yarumos, and the Cable Aéreo station without relying on taxis. It also positions you perfectly for day trips to Los Nevados National Park or the coffee towns south of the city.

Practical Driving Information

Climate & Altitude

Manizales sits at 2,150 m elevation. Average temperature 14–18°C (57–64°F). Bring warm layers, especially for mornings and evenings. Rain is frequent, so keep a jacket handy.

Steep Roads

Manizales is one of Colombia's steepest cities. All roads are paved, but some hills are quite sharp. All our vehicles handle them well. Drive cautiously in rain.

Parking

Paid parking lots are available near the Cathedral, Chipre, and major shopping areas. Look for signs saying "Parqueadero." Costs are very affordable compared to international standards.

Best Time to Visit

January–February and August are popular times. Clear mornings give the best volcano views from Chipre. The annual Feria de Manizales (January) brings festivals and bullfighting traditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a 4x4 to drive around Manizales?

No. For the city itself, any vehicle works fine. However, if you plan to continue to Los Nevados National Park, a 4x4 like our Toyota Fortuner or Hilux is strongly recommended.

How many days do I need in Manizales?

One to two days covers the main city sights: Cathedral, Cable Car, and Chipre. If you want to explore the wider region including Los Nevados and the coffee zone, plan three or more days.

What are the Cable Car hours?

The Cable Aéreo schedule changes periodically. Check the current schedule before visiting. The ride connects Manizales with Villamaría and offers panoramic mountain views.

Do you accept international driving licenses?

Yes. We accept international driving licenses from any country, including all EU nations. We also accept payments in USD and offer bilingual service.

What are the best viewpoints in Manizales?

The three best viewpoints are Chipre (panoramic view of the Andes and city), the Cable Aéreo gondola (aerial views during the ride), and the Cathedral tower (views from inside the church). All are accessible by any car.
