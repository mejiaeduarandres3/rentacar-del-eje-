How to Get to Santa Rosa Hot Springs by Car

From Pereira (Matecaña Airport)

35 km via the Pereira–Santa Rosa road, then up into the mountains. Paved all the way. Estimated time: 40 minutes. Pick up your car at Matecaña Airport.

From Armenia (El Edén Airport)

85 km via Armenia–Pereira–Santa Rosa. Well-maintained highway. Estimated time: 1 hour 20 minutes. Pick up at El Edén Airport.

From Manizales

100 km via the Autopista del Café. Scenic mountain highway with good pavement. Estimated time: 1 hour 30 minutes. Pick up at La Nubia Airport.

From Santa Rosa de Cabal Town

15 km uphill from the town center. Paved road with mountain curves. Estimated time: 20 minutes. Stop in town first to try the famous chorizo santarrosano.

What to See & Do at Santa Rosa Hot Springs

Termales de Santa Rosa

The most iconic hot springs in Colombia's Coffee Region. A natural waterfall cascades from a cliff face directly into warm thermal pools surrounded by lush cloud forest. The sight of steaming water pouring over moss-covered rocks is unforgettable. Several pools at different temperatures let you find your perfect spot, from gently warm to genuinely hot. The complex includes changing rooms, lockers, a restaurant, and shaded rest areas. It is the most visited thermal destination in the region, so arriving early on weekdays gives you a quieter experience.

  • Natural waterfall into thermal pools
  • Check current prices before visiting
  • Parking available on site

Termales San Vicente

A more exclusive eco-lodge experience set deeper in the mountains. San Vicente offers a quieter, more intimate atmosphere compared to the main Termales. The thermal pools are nestled among native forest, and overnight guests enjoy the springs after day visitors leave. The lodge features comfortable rooms, a spa, and hiking trails through the surrounding cloud forest. If you want a peaceful, nature-immersed soak without the crowds, San Vicente is the place to go.

  • Eco-lodge with overnight stays available
  • Check current prices before visiting
  • Quieter and more exclusive

Santa Rosa de Cabal Town

Before or after the hot springs, stop in Santa Rosa de Cabal town. It is famous throughout Colombia for its chorizo santarrosano — grilled sausages served with arepas at roadside stalls along the main street. The town also has a charming central plaza, a beautiful church, and several traditional restaurants. With your own car, you can explore the town at your own pace and then drive up to the hot springs whenever you are ready.

Practical Driving Information

Parking

Both Termales de Santa Rosa and Termales San Vicente have parking lots available for visitors. Your car will be safe while you enjoy the pools.

Weather & Altitude

The hot springs sit at approximately 2,200 m elevation. Temperatures range from 14–20°C (57–68°F). Bring a light jacket — the mountain air feels cool between soaks.

Gas Stations

Fill up in Santa Rosa de Cabal town or Pereira before heading to the hot springs. There are no gas stations on the mountain road itself.

Best Time to Visit

The hot springs are enjoyable year-round — rain actually makes the experience more atmospheric. Weekday mornings are less crowded. Driest months: December–February and June–August.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a 4x4 to reach the hot springs?

No. The roads to both Termales de Santa Rosa and Termales San Vicente are paved. Any vehicle in our fleet will get you there without issues.

How far are the hot springs from Pereira?

About 40 minutes by car from Pereira airport. The road passes through Santa Rosa de Cabal town and continues into the mountains.

What is the difference between Termales de Santa Rosa and San Vicente?

Termales de Santa Rosa is famous for its natural waterfall cascading into thermal pools and is the most visited. Termales San Vicente is a more exclusive eco-lodge with a quieter, nature-immersed atmosphere.

Do you accept international driving licenses?

Yes. We accept international driving licenses from any country, including all EU nations. We also accept payments in USD and offer bilingual service.

Can I visit the hot springs and return the same day?

Absolutely. Most visitors make a day trip from Pereira or Santa Rosa de Cabal. With your own rental car, you have complete flexibility to arrive early and leave whenever you like.

What should I bring?

Bring a swimsuit, towel, change of clothes, and a light jacket for the mountain air. The altitude is around 2,200 m, so evenings can be cool even though the pools are warm.
