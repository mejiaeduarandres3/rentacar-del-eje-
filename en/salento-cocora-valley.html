Why Drive to Salento?

The most visited destination in Colombia's Coffee Region

🌴

Cocora Valley

Home to the world's tallest palm trees — the Colombian wax palm. A stunning 4-5 hour hike through cloud forest and towering palms.

☕

Coffee Farms

Tour working coffee farms and learn the process from bean to cup. Many farms offer English-speaking guides and tastings.

🏘

Colonial Town

Salento's colorful main street is lined with artisan shops, restaurants, and viewpoints. Try the famous local trout and fresh coffee.

🚗

Easy Drive

Just 45 minutes from Pereira and 30 minutes from Armenia. Well-paved roads all the way to Salento.
