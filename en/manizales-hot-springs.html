How to Get to the Hot Springs by Car

Termales El Otoño from Manizales

30 km on paved road heading east from Manizales. Family-friendly destination accessible with any vehicle. Estimated time: 45 minutes. Pick up your car at Manizales.

Termales del Ruiz from Manizales

60 km via the road toward Nevado del Ruiz. Mountain road, partially unpaved. A 4x4 is strongly recommended. Estimated time: 1 hour 30 minutes. The route passes through stunning páramo landscape.

From La Nubia Airport

La Nubia is Manizales' airport. From there, El Otoño is about 50 minutes and Termales del Ruiz about 1 hour 40 minutes. Pick up at La Nubia Airport.

From Pereira

130 km via the Autopista del Café to Manizales, then onward to the hot springs. Estimated time: about 2 hours to El Otoño. Pick up at Matecaña Airport.

What to See & Do at the Hot Springs

Termales del Ruiz

Perched at approximately 3,500 meters above sea level, Termales del Ruiz offers one of the most dramatic hot spring experiences in Colombia. The pools are heated by the volcanic activity of Nevado del Ruiz, and the surrounding landscape is raw Andean páramo — misty, windswept, and otherworldly. On clear days you can see the snow-capped peak of the volcano itself. The road up is an adventure in its own right, winding through cloud forest that gradually gives way to the high-altitude grasslands. A car is essential here since there is no reliable public transport to these remote pools. Bring very warm clothing — the air temperature often drops to 5°C (41°F) even when the pool water is pleasantly warm.

  • Altitude: ~3,500 m — volcanic-heated pools
  • 4x4 recommended (Fortuner or Hilux)
  • Check current prices before visiting

Termales El Otoño

A family-friendly thermal resort located on the eastern outskirts of Manizales at a comfortable altitude of about 2,000 meters. El Otoño features multiple pools at different temperatures, from warm wading areas for children to hotter pools for adults seeking a deep soak. The complex is well-maintained with changing rooms, a restaurant, picnic areas, and shaded gardens. The paved road from Manizales makes it accessible with any vehicle in our fleet. It is an ideal half-day or full-day escape, especially if you want a relaxing thermal experience without the high-altitude mountain drive that Termales del Ruiz requires.

  • Family-friendly, accessible by any car
  • Multiple pools at different temperatures
  • Check current prices before visiting

Manizales City

While in Manizales, take time to explore this vibrant Andean city. Visit the Catedral de Manizales for panoramic views from its tower, walk through the cable car system (Cable Aéreo), and try the local cuisine at the Recinto del Pensamiento botanical gardens. Manizales is also the gateway to Los Nevados National Park and the Nevado del Ruiz volcano.

Practical Driving Information

Vehicle Choice

For Termales del Ruiz, rent a Toyota Fortuner or Hilux — the mountain road can be rough and muddy. For El Otoño, any vehicle including the Kia Picanto works perfectly.

Weather & Clothing

Termales del Ruiz: 5–12°C (41–54°F), bring warm layers and a waterproof jacket. El Otoño: 16–22°C (61–72°F), a light sweater is enough.

Gas Stations

Fill up in Manizales before heading to either hot spring. There are no gas stations on the mountain roads beyond the city.

Road Conditions

El Otoño: paved all the way, good condition year-round. Termales del Ruiz: partially unpaved, best in dry season (Dec–Feb, Jun–Aug). Drive slowly on mountain curves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a 4x4 for Termales del Ruiz?

A high-clearance vehicle like the Toyota Fortuner or Toyota Hilux is strongly recommended for Termales del Ruiz. The mountain road is unpaved in sections and can be muddy. Termales El Otoño is accessible with any vehicle.

How cold is it at Termales del Ruiz?

Termales del Ruiz sits at approximately 3,500 m altitude. Air temperature ranges from 5–12°C (41–54°F). The pool water is warm from volcanic heating, but bring very warm clothing for before and after your soak.

Can I visit both hot springs in one day?

It is possible but tiring. Termales El Otoño is 45 minutes from Manizales while Termales del Ruiz is about 1 hour 30 minutes in the opposite direction. We recommend dedicating a separate day to each.

Do you accept international driving licenses?

Yes. We accept international driving licenses from any country, including all EU nations. We also accept payments in USD and offer bilingual service.

Is Termales El Otoño suitable for families?

Yes. Termales El Otoño is very family-friendly with multiple pools at different temperatures, a restaurant, changing facilities, and well-maintained grounds. It is easily accessible by any car.

When is the best time to visit?

Both hot springs can be enjoyed year-round. For Termales del Ruiz, drier months (December–February, June–August) mean better road conditions. El Otoño is comfortable in any weather.
