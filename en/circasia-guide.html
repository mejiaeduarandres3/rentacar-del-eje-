What to See & Do in Circasia

Cementerio Libre

Circasia's most distinctive landmark and one of the most unusual cemeteries in the world. Founded in 1933 by a group of free-thinkers, the Cementerio Libre (Free Cemetery) is a place where people of all faiths, beliefs, and philosophies are buried side by side without religious distinction. Unlike traditional Colombian cemeteries that are consecrated by the Catholic Church, this cemetery was created as a symbol of tolerance and intellectual freedom. Walking through its grounds, you will find graves marked with Masonic symbols, secular inscriptions, personal philosophies, and religious imagery from multiple traditions — all coexisting peacefully. It is a powerful, thought-provoking place that speaks to Colombia's diversity. The cemetery is free to enter and takes about 30–45 minutes to explore thoughtfully.

  • Free admission
  • Allow 30–45 minutes
  • Walking distance from the main square

Plaza Principal & Architecture

Circasia's main square is surrounded by colorful bahareque buildings — the traditional wattle-and-daub construction style that defines Coffee Region architecture. The facades are painted in vibrant colors, and many house small coffee shops, artisan workshops, and local restaurants. The town has a quieter, more local atmosphere compared to the more tourist-oriented Salento, which makes it a genuine window into everyday Quindío life. Grab a tinto (black coffee) at one of the cafés and watch the town go about its day.

Viewpoints & Panoramas

Circasia sits on elevated ground offering panoramic views of the Quindío valley and the surrounding coffee-covered hills. Several viewpoints on the outskirts of town provide sweeping vistas of the green landscape stretching toward the Andes. On clear days, you can see across the valley to the mountains beyond. These spots are especially beautiful in the golden light of late afternoon. With your own car, you can easily drive to the viewpoints and find your own quiet lookout spot.

Practical Driving Information

Parking

Public parking is available near the main square. The town is compact and walkable once you park. The Cementerio Libre also has parking space nearby.

Weather

Circasia sits at 1,790 m elevation. Average temperature 18–24°C (64–75°F). Pleasant year-round. Bring a light jacket for cooler evenings.

Time Needed

Half a day covers the Cementerio Libre, main square, coffee shops, and viewpoints. Circasia is ideal as a stop between Armenia, Filandia, and Salento.

Gas Stations

Gas stations are available on the Armenia–Circasia road. Fill up in Armenia or Circasia before heading to Filandia or Salento.
